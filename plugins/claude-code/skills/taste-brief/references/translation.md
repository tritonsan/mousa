# From qualities to design layers

Qloo names qualities; you turn them into concrete design moves. The translation is yours, so it is labelled as interpretation, and it must be specific enough that someone else could check it in the files.

## Weak and strong

An adjective is not a design move.

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| "a warm, minimal feel" | "aged-paper ground #f3ead8 with one rust accent #a8552f on links only" |
| "calm motion" | "sections drift in over 1.2 s with cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), and nothing moves faster" |
| "an artistic illustration" | "a stepped-terrace line drawing as an inline SVG of 24 paths, `terrace-figure`" |
| "a friendly voice" | "headlines of six words or fewer, no exclamation marks, a pause before the call to action" |

## Layer by layer

| Layer | Look at | Translate into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **palette** | look qualities, then surfaces | Hues, contrast and temperature: Atmospheric toward hazy, low-contrast mid-tones; Raw toward unbleached grounds and one hard accent. Never take a work's, brand's or team's own colours. |
| **typography** | look and mood qualities | Class and texture: Minimalist toward a geometric or grotesque sans with generous spacing; Nostalgic toward a book serif or a typewriter face; Monumental toward condensed capitals. Use library families, loaded from files. |
| **motion** | pacing tags, then tone | Tempo, easing and what moves: long, gliding eases for a stately or Pensive quality; quick, staccato cuts for a frenetic one; nothing that loops forever without a pause control. |
| **imagery and illustration** | surfaces and art styles | Line, form, texture and composition: layered, interlocking ornament for an ornate quality; riveted, gridded linework for an industrial one; stepped volumes for Sculptural. Draw or describe it yourself; never trace a logo, character or artwork. |
| **voice** | tone, then mood and meaning | Register, rhythm and vocabulary: short, plain lines with pauses for a hushed or Reflective tone; chorus-length, rallying lines for an anthemic one. Write your own lines, never a work's lyrics, dialogue or slogans. |
| **structure and layout** | meaning qualities, then works | What the design is organised around: Routine toward a week or a daily loop; Identity toward chapters of who someone becomes; Community toward a shared board or map. |
| **interaction** | mood and meaning | One behaviour that carries a quality, such as a slow reveal for Introspective or a remix control for a playful, collage-like taste. |

## Qualities first, works as evidence

Anchor palette and type to a quality, and cite a work beside it when the work sharpens it: Atmospheric, as Perfect Days carries it. The quality sets the colours, and the work explains which atmosphere you mean.

## When the surprises disagree with your sketch

Your frozen sketch is what you would have made without the data. Where a measured surprise points elsewhere, follow the surprise: new colours far from the sketch's, a type system outside its fonts, and at least one new section or component. A device anchored to your own confirmed prediction may keep the sketch's choice, so the difference shows exactly what the data changed.

## Keep the facts

Taste changes how the product looks, moves and speaks, never what it is.
- Keep every fact, price, name and protected file.
- A description keeps what an item is and does, and adds no feature, award, review or claim.
