# From qualities to design layers

Qloo names qualities; you turn them into concrete design moves. The translation is yours, so it is labelled as interpretation, and it must be specific enough that someone else could check it in the files.

## Weak and strong

An adjective is not a design move.

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| "a warm, minimal feel" | "aged-paper ground #f3ead8 with one rust accent #a8552f on links only" |
| "calm motion" | "sections drift in over 1.2 s with cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), and nothing moves faster" |
| "an artistic illustration" | "a stepped-terrace line drawing as an inline SVG of 24 paths, `terrace-figure`" |
| "a friendly voice" | "headlines of six words or fewer, no exclamation marks, a pause before the call to action" |

## Through the works' genre

A quality says what the audience's works share; their genre says how such works look. The same word lands differently in different genres, and the genre is what makes the design this audience's:

| Quality | In one genre | In another |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atmospheric | long-lens landscape stills: hazy, low-contrast mid-tones, the horizon low | a warehouse rave flyer: deep black, one acid colour, type lit as if by a strobe |
| Bold Typography | a sports broadcast: boxed condensed capitals on a dark score bug, tabular figures | a protest poster: hand-cut wood type, two inks, words stacked edge to edge |
| Intimate | an R&B sleeve: one close, warm portrait and very small type | a stand-up special's poster: a single spotlight on a brick wall, the comic's name in a marquee face |

In your reading, give each cluster 1-3 **conventions**: the visual habits its works share as a genre or medium, named as a designer who knows those works would name them. Then let the palette or the type translate one of them. A convention is a genre's habit, never one brand's logo, mascot, colours, packaging or trade dress.

## Leave the house style behind

Models that design fall into a few looks when nothing else decides, whatever the audience: a warm cream ground, a condensed display serif with one italic word in an accent colour, small uppercase monospace labels after a short hairline, numbered sections and pill buttons; a near-black page lit by one amber lamp; or violet gradients, frosted cards and one neutral sans. `design_check` finds these signatures and a page keeps at most two. If your translation of a quality lands on one, it came from the model, not the audience: go back to the works' genre.

## Layer by layer

| Layer | Look at | Translate into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **palette** | look qualities, then surfaces, through a convention | Hues, contrast and temperature as the works' genre uses them: Atmospheric in landscape stills toward hazy mid-tones, in a rave flyer toward black and one acid colour; Raw in a zine toward photocopier grey and one hard accent. Never take a work's, brand's or team's own colours. |
| **typography** | look and mood qualities, through a convention | Class and texture as the genre sets type: Bold Typography in a broadcast toward condensed capitals in boxes, in a varsity jacket toward a collegiate slab; Nostalgic in a paperback toward a book serif, in a diner toward a chrome script; Monumental in civic signage toward tall capitals. Use library families, loaded from files. |
| **motion** | pacing tags, then tone | Tempo, easing and what moves: long, gliding eases for a stately or Pensive quality; quick, staccato cuts for a frenetic one; nothing that loops forever without a pause control. |
| **imagery and illustration** | surfaces and art styles | Line, form, texture and composition: layered, interlocking ornament for an ornate quality; riveted, gridded linework for an industrial one; stepped volumes for Sculptural. Draw or describe it yourself; never trace a logo, character or artwork. |
| **voice** | tone, then mood and meaning | Register, rhythm and vocabulary: short, plain lines with pauses for a hushed or Reflective tone; chorus-length, rallying lines for an anthemic one. Write your own lines, never a work's lyrics, dialogue or slogans. |
| **structure and layout** | meaning qualities, then works | What the design is organised around: Routine toward a week or a daily loop; Identity toward chapters of who someone becomes; Community toward a shared board or map. |
| **interaction** | mood and meaning | One behaviour that carries a quality, such as a slow reveal for Introspective or a remix control for a playful, collage-like taste. |

## Qualities first, through a convention

Anchor palette and type to a quality, and cite a work beside it when the work sharpens it: Atmospheric, as Perfect Days carries it. The quality sets what to say, the convention of that work's genre sets how, and the work explains which atmosphere you mean.

## When the surprises disagree with your sketch

Your frozen sketch is what you would have made without the data. Where a measured surprise points elsewhere, follow the surprise: new colours far from the sketch's, a type system outside its fonts, and at least one new section or component. A device anchored to your own confirmed prediction may keep the sketch's choice, so the difference shows exactly what the data changed.

## Keep the facts

Taste changes how the product looks, moves and speaks, never what it is.
- Keep every fact, price, name and protected file.
- A description keeps what an item is and does, and adds no feature, award, review or claim.
- Invent no fact. No price or "free", amenity, offer or schedule the brief does not state, however well it fits the voice.
